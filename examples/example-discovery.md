# Example: Completed Discovery Doc

This is a worked example of what `docs/ai-dlc/discovery.md` looks like once Phase 01 is actually complete —
for a fictional small business site, "Riverside Veterinary Clinic." It exists purely as a reference for how
much detail a "done" phase doc should have; it is not part of any real project and should not be copied
verbatim into a real `docs/ai-dlc/discovery.md`.

## Business

| # | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What problem does the website solve? | Current site has no online booking; the clinic gets 30+ phone calls/day just to schedule appointments. |
| 2 | Who is the target audience? | Local pet owners within a 15-mile radius. |
| 3 | What markets/countries are targeted? | Single market — one US city/region. |
| 4 | What languages are required? | English only. |
| 5 | What are the primary business goals? | Reduce phone booking volume; showcase services; build trust via reviews. |
| 6 | What actions should visitors take? | Book an appointment; call for emergencies; read service pages. |
| 7 | What makes the business different? | Only 24-hour emergency vet in the region. |
| 8 | Are there legal/compliance requirements? | HIPAA-style client confidentiality isn't applicable (not human health), but standard privacy policy for any collected contact data is required. |
| 9 | Are there accessibility requirements? | WCAG 2.1 AA requested by the owner. |
| 10 | Are there brand restrictions? | Must use existing logo and the clinic's established green/blue palette. |

## Audience

| # | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | Who are the primary user types? | Existing clients (repeat bookings), new clients (first visit), emergency visitors. |
| 12 | What are their main tasks? | Book/reschedule an appointment, find emergency contact info, read about services/pricing. |
| 13 | What devices do they commonly use? | ~70% mobile based on current analytics. |
| 14 | Are there different journeys for different audiences? | Yes — emergency visitors need the phone number and address above the fold; new clients need service info first. |

## Existing Website

| # | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 15 | What currently works well? | Service descriptions are well-written and should be preserved largely as-is. |
| 16 | What currently causes problems? | No mobile-friendly booking flow; contact form doesn't send email reliably. |
| 17 | What must not change? | Clinic's phone number and physical address (already indexed everywhere). |
| 18 | What must be improved? | Booking flow, mobile performance, contact form reliability. |
| 19 | What content/assets must be preserved? | Team bios, existing service photos. |
| 20 | What historical SEO value must be preserved? | Current URLs rank for "emergency vet [city]" — must 301 redirect if URLs change. |

## Success

| # | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 21 | How will project success be measured? | 50% reduction in booking-related phone calls within 3 months of launch. |
| 22 | What are the critical user journeys? | Book an appointment; find emergency contact fast. |
| 23 | What are launch blockers? | Booking flow must work end-to-end; existing top-ranking URLs must redirect correctly. |
| 24 | What is explicitly out of scope? | Online payment processing (phase 2, not this project). |
